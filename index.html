Sikander Cinema AI Profile Blueprint

This behavioral vector profile governs how movies are scored and recommended across Sikander OS.

Core Mathematical Taste Pillars

  • Pillar 1: Dark / Cynical Realism (Weight: 25%) — Flawed systems, institutional corruption, raw human consequence.
  • Pillar 2: Plausible High-Concept Sci-Fi (Weight: 20%) — Hard science foundations, conceptual ingenuity, mind-bending causality.
  • Pillar 3: Psychological Tension & Moral Ambiguity (Weight: 20%) — High-stakes ethical dilemmas, dread over jump scares.
  • Pillar 4: High-Agency Protagonists (Weight: 15%) — Competence, resourcefulness, deliberate strategy vs passive victims.
  • Pillar 5: Airtight Narrative Architecture (Weight: 10%) — Chekhov's guns resolved, logical coherence, earned twists.
  • Pillar 6: Visual & Atmospheric Distinctiveness (Weight: 10%) — Immersive cinematography, evocative sound design, singular aesthetic.

Instant Red Flags (Score Penalties)

Unearned melodrama, cheap jump scares, preachy moralizing, plot armor, unaddressed continuity holes, and slow-burn without substantive payoff.
