💾 100% Free Offline Playback via Spotify "Local Files"

If you want Spotify to play your exact downloaded .mp3 files directly from your hard drive without relying on whether the song is on Spotify's public streaming service:

1

Enable Local Files in Spotify Desktop

Open Spotify on your PC -> Click your Profile picture -> Settings (or press Ctrl + P). Scroll down to "Local Files" and toggle ON "Show Local Files".

2

Add "I:\Music" as a Source

Click "Add a source" and select your I:\Music folder. Instantly, all 2,500+ tracks will show up in Spotify under "Your Library" -> "Local Files"!

3

Syncing Local Files to Your Phone (Wi-Fi Transfer)

1. Connect your phone and PC to the same home Wi-Fi network.
2. On phone Spotify app: Go to Settings -> Local Files -> Toggle ON "Show audio files from this device".
3. Open your playlists on your phone and tap the green Download arrow. Spotify transfers the audio files over Wi-Fi so they play on your phone even offline in the car or gym!
