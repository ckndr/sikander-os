Sikander Audio Taste DNA Blueprint

Mathematical model and 6-pillar weighting system governing music recommendations.

Core Mathematical Taste Pillars

  • Pillar 1: Sunset Deep House & Saxophone Melodic Chill (Weight: 25%) — 118–124 BPM. Organic saxophone, acoustic piano, breezy summer euphoria (Nora En Pure, Klingande, Ehrling, Mahmut Orhan, De Hofnar).
  • Pillar 2: Hypnotic Progressive Trance & Sunset Balearic (Weight: 20%) — 128–136 BPM. Expansive synth arpeggios, ethereal breakdowns, late-night highway flow state (Chicane, Deadmau5, Eric Prydz, Tiësto, Alpha 9, Blood Groove & Kikis).
  • Pillar 3: High-Energy Gym & Peak Electronic Drive (Weight: 20%) — 126–132 BPM. Driving 4-on-the-floor kick, motivating builds, PR overload fuel (Avicii, Calvin Harris, David Guetta, Zedd, Don Omar).
  • Pillar 4: Soulful Desi Heritage & Melodic Sufi / Pop (Weight: 15%) — 85–115 BPM. Emotive Pakistani rock, classical Sufi qawwali, Coke Studio, Urdu/Hindi acoustics (Fuzon, Junoon, NFAK remixes, Atif Aslam, Kailash Kher).
  • Pillar 5: Atmospheric Lounge, Buddha Bar & World Chillout (Weight: 10%) — 80–105 BPM. Downtempo world groove, sitar, ethnic acoustic strings, evening contemplation (Buddha-Bar, Blank & Jones, Schiller, Moby).
  • Pillar 6: 90s-2000s Nostalgic Dance-Pop & Melodic Alt-Rock (Weight: 10%) — 112–128 BPM. Timeless millennial hooks, Eurodance, singalong anthems (Modjo, Sash!, Edward Maya, Coldplay, The Script).

Source Hierarchy & Weighting

Tier 1 Manual Year-Month Archives (1.5x Weight): 26 monthly folders from 2016 to 2020 representing individually chosen downloads.
Tier 1 Desi Folder (1.5x Weight): Hand-selected cultural soul anchor.
Tier 1 Spotify Playlists (1.3x–1.4x Weight): Active Liked songs, Gym playlist, Trance playlist.
Tier 2 Compilations (1.0x–1.2x Weight): Deep 2019, Sax House, Buddha Bar, ISOS.

Strict Red Flags (Automatic Score Penalties)

Monotone autotuned mumble rap, chaotic screamo with no harmonic resolution, repetitive soul-less minimal loops, and disposable TikTok meme audio.
